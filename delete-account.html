Delete your LinkTracker account

App: LinkTracker (com.js7softwares.linktracker) · Developer: JS7 Softwares

You can delete your LinkTracker account and all the data linked to it at any time. Choose one of the two options below.

Option 1: Delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open LinkTracker.
  2. Tap the Settings icon.
  3. Scroll to Account and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Your account and data are deleted straight away.

Tip: use Settings › Export my links first if you want to keep a copy of your links.

Option 2: Ask us by email

If you no longer have the app, email us from the address you used to sign in:

To: js7softwares@gmail.com
Subject: Delete my LinkTracker account
Include: the email address of your account

We may reply to confirm the request comes from the account owner. We delete the account and its data within 30 days of the request.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address, user ID and Google profile details)
  • All your saved links, notes, thumbnails and categories, including private links
  • Categories you shared with others, and the list of people you shared them with
  • Your email address from other people's sharing lists, so your access to their shared categories ends
  • Your private-links PIN, plan details and usage events

What is kept

  • Links you added to someone else's shared category belong to that category's owner and stay in their account.
  • Subscription payments are handled by Google Play, which keeps its own purchase records. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in the Play Store under Payments & subscriptions.
  • Advertising data collected by Google AdMob is managed by Google. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings.

No other data is kept after deletion. Accounts used without signing in are also deleted automatically after 30 days of not opening the app.
