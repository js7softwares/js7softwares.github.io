Privacy Policy

App: LinkTracker (com.js7softwares.linktracker) · Developer: JS7 Softwares · Effective date: 17 November 2026

This policy explains what information LinkTracker collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using LinkTracker you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: if you create an account, your email address and a unique user ID. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and the basic profile details Google provides (your name and profile picture); we never see your Google password. If you choose “Continue without an account”, we create an anonymous user ID instead.
  • Content you save: the links you save, their titles, thumbnails and previews, the categories you create, and any notes you add.
  • Sharing: if you share a category, we store the email addresses you enter, the access you give each person (view only or add links), and your own email address so they can see who shared it. If someone shares a category with you, we use your email address to show it to you.
  • Usage events: the app records simple events linked to your user ID, such as starting the app without an account, reaching a free-plan limit, exporting or importing links, sharing a category, or opening the upgrade screen. We use these only to understand how features are used and to improve the app.
  • Private links PIN: if you use the Premium private-links feature, the PIN is used only to lock those links inside the app.
  • Advertising data: the free version shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device’s advertising ID, IP address, and basic device and usage information to show and measure ads.
  • Purchase information: subscriptions are processed by Google Play. We receive confirmation of your subscription status but never see your payment card or bank details.

2. How we use your information

  • To save, organise, search and sync your links across your devices.
  • To fetch link previews (title, thumbnail, platform icon) and suggest categories.
  • To send reminders you set up and to check whether saved posts are still available.
  • To let you share categories with people you choose, and to show you categories shared with you.
  • To import links from a backup file you choose, and to create export files.
  • To provide Premium features and verify your subscription.
  • To understand which features are used and improve the app.
  • To show ads to free users.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Sharing categories with others

  • Sharing is optional and needs a Google sign-in. Only the people whose email addresses you add can see a shared category.
  • People you share with can see the links, titles, thumbnails and notes in that category, and your email address. Private links are never shared, even if they are in a shared category.
  • If you give someone “add links” access, the links they add are stored in your account and count towards your plan.
  • You can change or remove someone’s access at any time, and anyone can leave a category shared with them. Access ends immediately.

4. Third-party services

LinkTracker uses these services, which have their own privacy policies:

  • Supabase: account sign-in and storage of your saved data
  • Google Sign-In: signing in with your Google account
  • Google AdMob: advertising (how Google uses data in ads)
  • Google Play Billing: subscription payments

5. Ads and consent

Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), we ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in the app under Settings › Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings. Premium subscribers do not see ads.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • Without an account: if you use the app without an account and don’t open it for 30 days, your data is deleted automatically. The app warns you in advance.
  • With an account: your data is kept until you delete your account.
  • Export and import: all users can export a backup of their links at any time. The export file is saved on your device and is not sent to us. When you import a file, only the links in it are saved to your account, like any other link.
  • Shared categories: when you stop sharing, leave a shared category, or delete your account, the sharing records are deleted. Links you added to someone else’s category stay in their account.
  • Deleting your account: you can delete your account and all its data from the app, or by emailing us at the address below. We delete your data within 30 days of the request.

7. Security

Your data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with access controls so that only you, and the people you choose to share a category with, can read your saved links. No method of storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

8. Children

LinkTracker is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new effective date.

10. Contact
